# Jain Metals Admin Panel Functionalities

This document outlines the features and capabilities of the current Jain Metals Administrative Panel.

## 1. Dashboard (Foundry Overview)
The dashboard provides a high-level summary of the store's performance.
- **Key Metrics**: Real-time display of Total Orders, Active Products, Total Customers, and Revenue (calculated from paid orders).
- **Navigation**: Quick access to all administrative modules via a sidebar.

## 2. Product Management
A central hub for managing the product catalog.
- **Catalog Overview**: Paginated list of products with search and filtering.
- **CRUD Operations**: Complete capability to Add, Edit, and Delete products.
- **Inventory Control**: Toggle product visibility (Active/Inactive).
- **Rich Content**: Manage product titles, descriptions, categories, and multiple images.
- **Pricing & Variants**: Configuration of base prices and product variations.

## 3. Order Management
Track and fulfill customer orders efficiently.
- **Order List**: Comprehensive view of all orders with status indicators (Payment, Fulfillment).
- **Advanced Filtering**: Search by order ID or status.
- **Detailed View**: Access specific order information, including items purchased, customer details, shipping address, and payment status.

## 4. Category Management
Organize the storefront for better customer navigation.
- **Manage Categories**: Create, rename, and delete product categories.
- **Assignment**: Interface to link products to specific categories.

## 5. Delivery Rates Configuration
Flexible shipping cost management tailored to geography.
- **Pincode-level Rates**: Set specific delivery charges for individual pincodes.
- **State-level Rates**: Define bulk rates for entire states to simplify regional shipping.
- **International Shipping**: Configure delivery costs for different countries.
- **Smart Selection**: Includes utility to fetch distinct states for easier configuration.

## 6. Promotions & Coupons
Drive sales with targeted discount strategies.
- **Coupon Codes**: Create custom codes (e.g., "WELCOME10") with fixed or percentage-based discounts.
- **Usage Rules**: Set minimum order values and validity periods for coupons.
- **Sale Prices**: Apply manual sale prices to individual products.
- **Bulk Category Discounts**: Apply percentage discounts across entire product categories at once.
- **Promotion Tracking**: Enable or disable promotions instantly.

## 7. Customer Management
Maintain a database of yours users.
- **Customer Directory**: View a complete list of registered customers.
- **Profile Details**: Access contact information and historic interaction data.
